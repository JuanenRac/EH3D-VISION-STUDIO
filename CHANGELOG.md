## [0.1.1] - Results in the convention of the server, the simulated station, search by mDNS and new readings

- **A picture of the window in the README:** the window with the example picture and design, written by the picture mode (`--shot`) of the published program, with its caption in the eight languages.
- **Results in the convention of the server (`convention`, `eh3d-vision server send fiducials|focus|blobs`):** a result is a file `vision-<kind>.json` of format `application/json` and schema `eh3d.vision.result/1` with `source` (width, height and the SHA-256 of the picture, made here with a SHA-256 of its own), `findings` of the kind (`fiducials` `{x, y, radius_px, score}`, `focus` `{score, sharp}`, `blobs` `{x, y, area_px}`, `barcode` `{format, text, x, y}` that is checked but never made), `units` `px`, `evidence` and `producer`, the same document that the `vision` plugin of EH3D-SERVER makes; it is kept with its `schema_version` and a document that does not follow the convention is refused before it is sent. `evidence` is `picture` unless `--evidence camera|simulator` says otherwise, since a file does not say that a camera made it. The score of a focus check is 50 when the sharpness is `focus_reference` (a new key, 100 by default), and the blobs are the spots of the picture above 20 pixels, the biggest first, at most `--max-findings`.
- **`eh3d-vision server check FILE.json`:** tells whether a document follows the convention (the schema, the kind, the picture, every finding of the kind inside the picture, the units, the evidence and the producer) and names the part that does not, as `findings[2]: x is inside the picture (0 to 640)`.
- **The simulated station of the server (`station`, `eh3d-vision server detect fiducials|barcode|focus|blobs IMAGE`):** builds the job for the `vision` plugin (`POST /api/v1/work/jobs` with `kind: detect`, the size and the hash of the picture, a `--seed` and, with `--artifact`, `output: artifact`), starts it, reads it until it ends and gives the result from the view of the job or the id of the artifact that the server made. `station::simulate` follows the plugin step by step (the same mix of the input, the same generator, the same order of the numbers) and `server::SimServer` answers the three routes of the jobs with it, so the whole chain is tested without a network; it has not been run against the real server.
- **The search for the server on the local network is a real switch (`eh3d-vision server discover`, feature `mdns`):** lists the `host:port` of the servers that answer to `_eh3d._tcp`; it asks nothing of the network while `server_mdns` is off (the default) and a program built without `--features mdns` says so. The release binaries are built with the feature. What is found are candidates, never connected to by themselves. A test points the resolver of the suite at a responder on the loopback; the workflow runs the clippy and the tests of that feature.
- **An edge to a fraction of a pixel, with its uncertainty (`edge`, `eh3d-vision edge IMAGE --from X,Y --to X,Y`):** the picture is read along the line (bilinear, `edge_step_px` apart), the two ends are the two flat grounds, the edge is where the profile crosses the middle level between two samples, and the answer has the position along the line and in the picture, the contrast, the slope, the width of the transition from 10 to 90 percent, the noise of the grounds and the uncertainty (one standard deviation: the noise with the rounding of 8 bits over the slope where the profile crosses). A line with no edge is `edge_flat`, a line that leaves the picture or is too short `edge_line`; the exit code is 1 above `edge_max_uncertainty_px` (new keys `edge_step_px` and `edge_max_uncertainty_px`).
- **The histogram and the automatic threshold (`histogram`, `eh3d-vision histogram IMAGE`):** the grey levels in 8 to 256 groups, the cuts of Otsu (checked equal to the one of the search for marks), isodata and the triangle method, the share of the variance that the two groups of Otsu explain, the percentiles, the share of black and white pixels and whether the three cuts agree within 8 levels.
- **A picture against a reference through a mask of tolerance (`reference`, `eh3d-vision refcheck REFERENCE PICTURE`):** a pixel of the mask at 0 is held to `diff_threshold`, from 1 to 254 is allowed that many levels more and 255 is not looked at; the answer has the pixels looked at and left out, those outside the tolerance, how much they pass it (mean and largest), the regions of at least `diff_min_area`, the box and the verdict against `diff_max_changed`; `--map FILE.pgm` writes the pixels outside the tolerance as a picture. A picture and a mask of different sizes are refused with `size_mismatch`, a mask that leaves nothing to look at with `reference_nothing_checked`.
- **The residuals of the lens, mark by mark (`lens::residual_report`, `eh3d-vision lens IMAGE --residuals`):** for each mark of the grid how far it lies from its place in the lattice before and after the correction, the mark that is farthest, the three bands of distance from the centre with their rms and the ratio of the outer band to the inner one, which says that two radial terms are not enough when it is well above 1; CSV or JSON with `--format`, to a file with `--output`.
- **Settings of the server, the state of the link and the search for servers in the window:** the side panel has the panel of the suite from `eh3d-view` (the six boxes, login, sessions, test of the connection), the badge of the link in the status bar, and the search for servers on the network (only with the switch of the applied settings on, in a thread, with the candidates as buttons that fill in the address). The window now depends on `eh3d-view` of EH3D-COMMON (only with the `gui` feature) and the workflows pin EH3D-COMMON to the v0.2.6 tag.
- **A histogram, the edge across a line, the check against a reference and a line of help in the window:** the histogram is drawn in 32 groups with the cut of Otsu, the line of an edge is four numbers with the step and the limit as fields, the check against a reference uses the second picture and an optional mask of tolerance (a new dialog), and each of these panels and the picture check, the lens and the comparison of two pictures has a closed line of help in the eight languages. The words are 34 new sentences in en, es, de, fr, it, ja, zh and pt (Portugal).
- **Tests of the new readings with a fixed seed (`tests/fuzz_camera.rs`, `tests/camera.rs`):** an edge across any line of any picture, a profile of any numbers, the histogram of any picture, a picture against a reference through any mask, the residuals of any marks, documents of the convention that are cut, flipped or filled with garbage, requests and answers of the station made of garbage, and documents made from any findings; and the new commands run as a program on pictures that the tests draw (the files, the exit codes and what is refused). The tests are 516 in `cargo test`.

## [0.1.0] - Server mode on the shared client, orientation, footprint, sharpness map, board scale and window panels

- **The server mode runs on the shared client of the suite (`eh3d-serverclient` of EH3D-COMMON, `SIBLING_REFS` pinned to the v0.2.5 tag in the workflows):** the keys keep their names and ranges (`server_address`, `server_token`, `server_machine`, `server_timeout_s`, `server_retries`) and `server_mdns` is new (off by default; nothing is asked of the network and no resolver is linked in). The retries, the state of the link, the time limits and the plain-HTTP warning are those of the shared client; a refusal of the server is described by its code (`describe`) and can be said in the eight languages of the table of the client. The server in memory (`server::SimServer`) now speaks the routes below through `handle` and the `Transport` of the client.
- **Routes that exist instead of routes that were guessed:** the server has no plugin of vision, so `server send KIND` keeps the JSON result of the command as an artifact (`POST /api/v1/artifacts`, name `vision-<kind>.json`, format `application/json`, the machine and the kind in its `params`, the producer `eh3d-vision`) and `server config` reads the newest artifact `vision-<machine>.cfg` of the format `text/x-eh3d-vision-config` (`GET /api/v1/artifacts?format=...` and `GET /api/v1/artifacts/{id}`), written as the lines of `vision.cfg` (comments and blank lines allowed, a key written twice is an error with its line) or as a JSON object; the rule that the lot is taken or none, and that `server_*` keys are never taken, is unchanged. `send` takes the four new kinds as well. Base64 for the body is written in the program.
- **Which way a part is held (`orientation::analyse`, `eh3d-vision orientation`):** the part is the biggest spot that differs from the board (its grey level is the median of the border of the picture), the mark is what inside it differs from the body, even when it looks like the board, and the result is the direction of the mark from the middle of the part (degrees counter-clockwise from the x axis, y up), the quarter of a turn it is nearest to, how far from it, and with `--expect-deg` how far from the wanted direction. Refused as `no_mark`, `centred`, `mark_big`, `wrong_way` or `skewed`; a picture with no part is `no_part`, a part cut by the edge `part_cut`. CSV or JSON (`format` `eh3d-vision-orientation`); the new keys are `orientation_body_contrast` (30), `orientation_mark_contrast` (40), `orientation_min_mark_px` (4) and `orientation_tolerance_deg` (10).
- **The footprint of the pads, each on its own (`footprint::judge`, `eh3d-vision footprint`):** every pad of the picture is judged against `--pad-length` and `--pad-width` with `--tolerance` (`footprint_tolerance_mm`, 0.05 mm): one line for each pad, rows from the top down and each from left to right, with the differences and the reasons `length` and `width`, and `--count` adds the reason `count`. The exit code is 1 when any pad is off; a picture with no pad is `no_pads`. CSV or JSON (`format` `eh3d-vision-footprint`).
- **A map of where the picture is sharp (`sharpmap::sharp_map`, `eh3d-vision sharpmap`):** the picture is cut into `--cells` by `--cells` cells (`sharpmap_cells`, 8) and each gets its sharpness (the variance of the Laplacian), its contrast and a state: `empty` when its contrast is below `sharpmap_min_contrast` (3), `weak` when its sharpness is below `--min-share` (`sharpmap_min_share`, 0.5) of the median, else `ok`. The result has the median, the weakest and the sharpest cell, the uniformity, the tilt of the sharpness along x and along y as a share of the median and the reason `uneven`; `--heat FILE.pgm` writes the picture of the map. CSV or JSON (`format` `eh3d-vision-sharpmap`).
- **The scale from a board of squares, with a report (`board::scale_from_squares`, `eh3d-vision board`):** from the squares that `squares` finds the turn of the board is worked out, the squares are put in rows and columns, and the median distance between neighbours along each axis and the side of the squares give the millimetres of a pixel along x, along y and from the side (`--pitch-mm` or `pitch_mm`, `--square-mm` or `board_square_mm`). Refused as `aspect` (the pixels are not square by more than `--max-aspect`, 0.02), `spread` (the distances differ by more than `--max-spread`, 0.05) or `disagree` (the side and the pitch do not give the same scale); a missing square does not move the pitch. CSV, JSON (`format` `eh3d-vision-board`) and, with `--report FILE.txt`, an account for a person ending with the line `mm_per_px = ...`.
- **Four more panels in the window (`Window::find_matches`, `rectify_picture`, `judge_row`, `compare_pictures`):** the search by correlation with its list of places, the picture straightened from the four marks that were found (`<picture>-rectified.pgm`), the row of pads judged from the measured parts (`<picture>-pads.csv`) and the comparison with a second picture that is opened apart (`<picture>-diff.csv` and `<picture>-changes.pgm`), with the values of `correlate_*`, `rectify_max_side`, `pads_tolerance` and `diff_*` as fields and every word in the eight languages. The files are never written over a file that is there unless `replace_output` says so, and a new picture forgets the old results. The logic is tested; the drawing has not been looked at on a screen.
- **Seeded random tests and more tests (`tests/fuzz.rs`, `tests/analysis.rs`):** random pictures, pads, squares and settings go through the four new readings, text of a configuration kept on the server and Base64 are damaged and round-tripped, and the panels of the window go through random pictures, all from a generator that reads no clock; the commands are run as programs on pictures that the tests draw, and the server mode is tested over the new routes and over the plain-HTTP transport on the loopback. The suite has 445 tests that run.

## [0.0.9] - Server mode, correlation marks, perspective correction, rows of pads and image comparison

- **The server of the machine (`server::Client`, `server::SimServer`, `eh3d-vision server health|config|send`):** the program can talk to the EH3D server with the keys the other programs of the suite use (`server_address`, `server_token`, `server_machine`, `server_timeout_s` 1 to 120 and `server_retries` 0 to 5, all in `vision.cfg` and `examples/vision.cfg`; an empty address means that there is no server and nothing is sent). `health` asks `GET /api/v1/health` without a token; `config` reads `GET /api/v1/machines/{machine}/vision/config`, takes the values that are keys of `vision.cfg` all or nothing (a value out of its range, or values that do not go together, refuse the lot; the keys `server_*` and the keys this program does not have are left out and counted, so a server cannot move the program to another address or change its token) and writes them as lines for the file (`--output`, never over a file without `--replace`); `send quality|offset|pads|diff` takes the arguments of the command of that name and posts its JSON result to `POST /api/v1/machines/{machine}/vision/results` as `{kind, machine, app, result}`, with the exit code 1 after the result was sent when its verdict is a fail. The retries follow the shared client of the suite: a connection that could not be made is tried again for any method, a time-out or a broken connection and an answer of 502, 503 or 504 only for `GET`, `HEAD`, `PUT`, `DELETE` and `OPTIONS` (a `POST` is never sent twice), with pauses of 200 ms, 400 ms... up to 2 s; a 401 is `unauthorized` and a 403 `forbidden`, not tried again; the errors carry the code and the detail of the server and the link is `Connected`, `Disconnected` or `LoggedOut`. The token is never printed or put in a result, and a token that would go over plain HTTP to an address that is not local is warned of. It is a thin layer with no new dependency, to be replaced by the shared client of the suite when it is published; the routes are the ones the `cam` plugin of the server will serve, so it is tested against a server in memory and a socket on the loopback, not against the real server. An answer whose status is not from 100 to 599 is refused.
- **Marks found by correlation (`correlate::correlate`, `eh3d-vision correlate`):** a disc of the wanted diameter is slid over the picture and the normalised cross-correlation of the grey levels with it is worked out at every place (an integral image gives the mean and the variance under the template), so a mark that the threshold loses on uneven light is found. A place is a mark when its correlation is the highest in its neighbourhood and at least `correlate_min_score` (0.8), the best first, none closer to a better one than a diameter; the centre is worked out to a fraction of a pixel from the three correlations around the top, `Dark` and `Light` marks are told apart, and a mark nearer to the edge than half the template is not found. A flat window has no shape and scores 0, a picture smaller than the template is `picture_small`, a search of more than three thousand million multiply-adds is `correlate_too_big`, and more marks than `max_parts` is `too_many_matches`. The new keys are `correlate_diameter` (20 pixels) and `correlate_min_score`; the options are `--diameter`, `--min-score`, `--light` and `--flatten`.
- **Perspective corrected (`perspective::rectify`, `perspective::Homography`, `eh3d-vision rectify`):** from the four corners of a rectangle in the picture (top left, top right, bottom right, bottom left) the homography that puts them on a true rectangle is worked out (eight equations, Gauss with the largest pivot) and the picture is drawn again as seen from straight above, with bilinear sampling and a fill for the places that come from outside the picture. The corners must be a convex four-sided shape (`bad_corners`), the size is the mean of the opposite sides unless `--size WxH` is given and no side may be below 2 or above `rectify_max_side` (4096, up to 16384; `bad_size`). The picture is written as a PGM file with `--output`, never over a file without `--replace`; `--fill` sets the grey level of the places outside.
- **A row of pads (`pads::measure_row`, `eh3d-vision pads`):** the parts of a picture are taken as the pads of a row and the line of best fit through their centres gives the pitch (the median distance between neighbours along it, with the least and the largest), how much it varies as a share of the median, the largest distance of a pad from the row, the direction of the row and the mean length and width. The verdict is `fail` with the reasons `pitch` (the pitches vary by more than `pads_tolerance`, 0.05), `row` (a pad lies off the row by more than that share of the pitch) and `expected` (the median pitch is not `--expect-pitch`, within the same share); fewer than three pads is `few_pads` and pads on top of each other `pads_overlap`. CSV (`pads,pitch_mm,pitch_min_mm,pitch_max_mm,spread,row_deviation_mm,row_angle_deg,length_mm,width_mm,verdict,reason`) or JSON (`format` `eh3d-vision-pads`); the exit code is 1 after the file is written when the row fails.
- **Two pictures compared, before and after (`diff::compare`, `eh3d-vision diff`):** the pixels whose grey level differs by more than `diff_threshold` (25) are changed; the result has their number and share, the mean and the largest difference, the box around them and the number of regions (four neighbours) of at least `diff_min_area` pixels (4), and the pictures count as the same when no more than `diff_max_changed` (0.01) of the pixels changed. `--mask FILE.pgm` writes the picture of the changes. CSV (`changed_px,total_px,changed_share,mean_abs,max_abs,regions,x0,y0,x1,y1,verdict`) or JSON (`format` `eh3d-vision-diff`); pictures of different sizes are `size_mismatch`, and the exit code is 1 when the pictures are not the same.
- **Tests with random input and a fixed seed (`tests/fuzz.rs`):** pictures of any size and grey levels go through the searches, the check of quality, the correlation and the comparison; corners anywhere go to `rectify`, random places to the row of pads, and configuration files, JSON objects and HTTP answers with random damage (cut, changed and inserted bytes, deeply nested lists, noise) are read or refused; the generator reads no clock, a failure says its seed and round, and the properties checked are that nothing panics and that what comes out makes sense (the scores are in range and the best first, a picture is never different from itself, what a server sends is taken all or nothing and never changes the link). New tests of the library and of the commands run on pictures that the tests draw, with a server on the loopback that answers with the server in memory.
- **Fix:** the writer of files has a form for bytes (`limit::write_bytes`) with the same rules as the one for text, so that a picture is never written over a file without `--replace` and never through a link.

## [0.0.8] - Pick offset panel with a table of corrections, picture quality check, mirrored cameras and nozzle axis

- **A panel in the window for where a picked part is held (`Window::offset_parts`, `offset_lines`, `save_offsets`):** the window works out the offset of every part of the open picture from the axis of the nozzle, the turn from the way it should be held and the correction that puts it right, judged against the package `inspect_package` of the table (the same rules as `eh3d-vision offset`), lists one line for each part in the language of the window (green when it passes, red with the reason when it does not: length, width, too far from the nozzle, turned too much) and saves the table as `<picture>-offsets.csv` beside the picture, never over a file that is there unless `replace_output` says so. The fields are `nozzle_x_mm`, `nozzle_y_mm`, `nominal_angle_deg`, `pick_max_offset_mm` and `pick_max_turn_deg`; a new picture forgets the table. The picture is not changed by the panel, and the parts of the Parts list are not either.
- **A table of corrections for a tray (`offset::pick_offsets`, `offset::to_table_csv`, `eh3d-vision offset --each`):** the offset of a picture with several parts, each on its own: the table has the column `part` (the number of the part, from 1, in the order of the picture) and then the columns of the file of one part, one row for each part. The exit code is 1 when any part fails, after the table is written, and the error output counts the parts that pass and that do not; `--each` writes CSV only (`--format json` with it is refused), and the form with one part still refuses a picture with more than one part (`parts_count`).
- **The turn of a part that is held right in the configuration file (`nominal_angle_deg`):** `--nominal-angle` was only an option of the command line and the settings of the offset that came from `vision.cfg` always started from 0; the value is now a key of the file, a field of the window panel and in `examples/vision.cfg`, and the option still overrides it.
- **A camera that sees the parts mirrored (`offset::mirror_parts`, `--mirror`, `pick_mirror`, a checkbox of the window panel):** for a camera that looks up from under the nozzle, or sees the part in a mirror, the x and the turn of each part change sign (the turn stays above -90 and up to 90, and turning twice gives the part back). The parts are turned and not the pixels, so that no half pixel is lost to one side of the centre of the picture; `offset`, `nozzle` and the window all use it.
- **The axis of the nozzle from the parts that were picked with it (`offset::estimate_nozzle`, `eh3d-vision nozzle IMAGE IMAGE...`):** from pictures of parts picked with the same nozzle (one part in each, at least two pictures, each refused with its file name when it has none or more than one) the command writes the two lines `nozzle_x_mm` and `nozzle_y_mm` for `vision.cfg`, the mean place of the parts in the picture, which does not depend on the axis that was in use when they were measured, and says on the error output how many parts it used, how spread they are (the standard deviation in x and in y), the distance of the farthest part from the mean place and the mean turn. A single part is the error `few_pickups`. The lines can be written straight to the file with `--output vision.cfg` (never over a file without `--replace`).
- **Is the picture good enough to measure from (`quality::measure`, `quality::reasons`, `eh3d-vision quality IMAGE`):** the sharpness (the variance of the Laplacian over the pixels that are not on the edge), the contrast (the standard deviation of the grey levels) and the mean grey level of a picture, as CSV (`sharpness,contrast,mean,verdict,reason`) or JSON (`format` `eh3d-vision-quality`). The picture is refused, with the exit code 1 after the file is written, as `flat` (the contrast is below `--min-contrast`), `dark` or `bright` (the mean is outside `--min-mean` and `--max-mean`) or `blurry` (the sharpness is below `--min-sharpness`; only when it is above 0 and the picture is not flat). The values depend on the camera and the lens, so they are keys of the configuration file (`quality_min_contrast`, `quality_min_mean`, `quality_max_mean`, `quality_min_sharpness`) and the sharpness starts off. A picture smaller than 3 by 3 pixels and settings that make no sense are refused (`picture_small`).
- **A poor picture is refused before a part is measured from it (`quality_check`, `quality::require_good`):** with `quality_check = yes`, `offset`, `nozzle` and the window panel check the picture as it was taken (before the light is flattened) and refuse it with `poor_picture`, the reasons joined with `+` and the numbers, instead of giving a part measured from a picture that cannot be trusted; it is off by default. The window has a panel for the check with the four values as fields, a checkbox for `quality_check`, a button that checks the open picture and a line with its numbers, and says in the status line, in the language of the window, whether the picture is good or why it is not.
- **Fix:** the checksum file of a release was named `SHA284SUMS` (and `.sha284`, `sha284sum`) in the eight READMEs, a number that had been changed by mistake together with the test count; it is `SHA256SUMS`, `.sha256` and `sha256sum` as the release makes them. The count of tests in the README, its tables and the architecture picture is now the number of tests that run.

## [0.0.7] - Reports in a thread, ring marks, lens factors and pick offset

- **Where a picked part is held, with the correction (`offset::pick_offset`, `eh3d-vision offset`):** a program that picks a part and places it needs to know how the part sits on the nozzle. From a picture of the part seen from below (one part in it) the command measures it as `measure` does, judges its size against the body of its package from the table as `inspect` does, and gives its offset from the axis of the nozzle (`--nozzle-x`, `--nozzle-y`, or `nozzle_x_mm` and `nozzle_y_mm`: millimetres from the centre of the picture, x to the right, y up), its turn from the way it should be held (`--nominal-angle`, counter-clockwise; a part as long as wide, within the tolerance, looks the same turned a quarter of a turn and gets the turn from -45 (not included) to 45, any other from -90 (not included) to 90), and the correction that puts it where the nozzle would have it, which is the same distances and turn the other way. The result is CSV (`x_offset_mm,y_offset_mm,rot_offset_deg,x_correction_mm,y_correction_mm,rot_correction_deg,length_mm,width_mm,package,delta_long_mm,delta_short_mm,verdict,reason`) or JSON (`format` `eh3d-vision-offset`), on the output or in `--output` (never written over a file without `--replace` or `replace_output`). The verdict is `fail`, with the reasons joined with `+`, when the length or the width is off the body by more than `--tolerance` (`inspect_tolerance`, the tolerance of the package wins), when the part is held farther than `--max-offset` (`pick_max_offset_mm`) or turned by more than `--max-turn` (`pick_max_turn_deg`; both 0 by default, not checked); the file is written and the exit code is 1. A picture with no part is refused with `no_parts` and one with more than one part with `parts_count`, because the part under the nozzle is alone in its picture. `--package` and the table come from `inspect_package` and `package_table` when they are not given. The new keys `nozzle_x_mm`, `nozzle_y_mm`, `pick_max_offset_mm` and `pick_max_turn_deg` are in `vision.cfg` and `examples/vision.cfg`. Tested on the numbers (the offset and the correction, the short way round for a part that looks the same turned and for a square, each reason, the files) and on the command line on a drawn part; no camera, nozzle or machine was used.
- **Ring marks of one size or of two, with a confidence (`ring::find_rings`, `eh3d-vision rings`, a section of the window):** a round mark with a hole (a donut) was not found by any search: the search for discs cuts the hole out of the area of the spot and the squares and crosses leave a ring out. A spot is now a ring when all its pixels lie between an inner and an outer radius around its centre, the band is full in each of 24 directions (a ring with a gap is not a ring), the hole over the outside is within `ring_min_ratio` and `ring_max_ratio` (0.25 and 0.85), the band is filled to at least `ring_min_fill` (0.85), the outline is round (`ring_max_elongation`, 1.25) and the ring is not cut by the edge; a filled disc, a ring that is not round and one whose hole is too small or too large are left out. The centre is the centre of the pixels, the score is the fill of the band times the roundness of the outline, and the confidence is that score lowered when the ring stands out only a little from the board around it (the contrast against `full_contrast`, the value of the other marks). When the rings are of two sizes each one is named small or large: `--split PX` or `ring_split` gives the outer diameter that tells them apart, and with 0 the diameters are sorted and cut at the widest gap between neighbours when the larger side of it is at least `ring_size_gap` times (1.25) the smaller (the cut is their geometric mean); rings of one size stay `single`. The command prints the place, the outer and inner diameter, the size class, the score and the confidence, takes `--light`, `--min-size`, `--max-size` and `--flatten` as the other searches do and refuses more spots than `max_parts`. The window has a section with the five values that matter (the range of the diameter and of the ratio and the split), a button, a list in the language of the window and the outer and inner circle drawn in a colour for each size. The new keys `ring_min_diameter`, `ring_max_diameter`, `ring_min_ratio`, `ring_max_ratio`, `ring_min_fill`, `ring_max_elongation`, `ring_split` and `ring_size_gap` are in `vision.cfg` and `examples/vision.cfg`, and the 13 new sentences are in the eight languages of `lang.rs`. Tested on drawn rings (their centre, their two diameters, a disc, a ring with a gap, a ring cut by the edge, a bad ratio, an ellipse, two sizes with the gap found and given, light rings, a weak ring that is trusted less, too many spots and settings that make no sense), in the window and on the command line; nothing has looked at a real mark or a real board.

## [0.0.1] - Fiducial marks in a camera image: detection, scale and the correction of a board

- **The marks:** Otsu threshold, connected spots, size, fill and elongation checks, marks cut by the edge left out, dark on light or light on dark.
- **The scale and the lens:** millimetres from a mark of known size, y going up, and the radial distortion recovered by least squares and corrected.
- **The board:** the turn, the shift and (if allowed) the size from two or more fiducials, with the residual; a wrong mark or a stretched board is not accepted.
- **The command line** `eh3d-vision` with `find` and `align`, and a sample picture and two sample files of points.
- **13 tests** run on a computer, with damaged input.

# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented here.

## [Unreleased]

- **A folder of pictures at once, with a report (`batch::check_picture`, `eh3d-vision batch`):** every PNG and PGM file of a folder (the other files, the folders and the links are left out) is read within `max_file_mib`, searched for marks (with `--light` and `--flatten` as in `find`) and gets one line: `file,status,marks,mean_score,min_score,mean_diameter_px,detail`, as CSV on the output or in the file of `--report`. The status is `ok`, `no_marks`, `picture_unreadable` or the code of the reader of files (`file_too_large`, `file_unreadable`), so one bad picture does not stop the rest; the exit code is 1 when a picture could not be used, after the report is written. A folder with more pictures than `batch_max_files` (500 by default) is refused with `too_many_files`; a report that is there is not written over without `--replace` (`file_exists`); and a file name that starts with `=`, `+`, `-` or `@` is written with a quote in front so that a spreadsheet does not run it as a formula. The new key `batch_max_files` is in `vision.cfg` and `examples/vision.cfg`.
- **The scale of the camera from the pitch of a target (`scale::from_pitch`, `eh3d-vision calibrate`):** the scale no longer has to come from the diameter of a single mark (which depends on where the edge is cut): the distance from each mark of a grid to its nearest neighbour is the pitch of the target, the median of them gives the millimetres of a pixel (one missing or extra mark does not move it), and the command prints the line `mm_per_px = ...` that `vision.cfg` and `measure` read. When the shortest and the longest of those distances differ by more than `pitch_tolerance` (5 % by default, or `--tolerance`) the answer is refused with the reason (a target not seen square-on, a lens that bends it, a wrong mark), fewer than 4 marks, two marks in one place and more than 2000 marks are refused too. The new key `pitch_tolerance` is in `vision.cfg` and `examples/vision.cfg`.
- **Cross-shaped marks are found (`cross::find_crosses`, `eh3d-vision crosses`):** a plus sign with its arms along the axes of the picture is a fiducial mark as good as a disc, and the search for discs left it out. A spot is a cross when its box is square, its area is what two arms of one width give (the width of the arm is worked out from it), the middle row and column of the box are full and the four corners of the box are empty; the centre is the centre of its pixels (exact on drawn crosses), the best score comes first, and a cross cut by the edge, a disc, a square, a line and a cross turned by 45 degrees are left out. Dark or light (`--light`), `--min-size` and `--max-size`, `--flatten`, and more spots than `max_parts` is refused with `too_many_parts`. The new keys `cross_min_size`, `cross_max_size` and `cross_max_elongation` are in `vision.cfg` and `examples/vision.cfg`.
- **Parts are measured for a program that checks a part before it is placed (`measure::measure_parts`, `eh3d-vision measure`):** each spot of the picture gives its centre (millimetres from the centre of the picture, x right and y up, the axes of the machine), the turn of its long side (-90 excluded to 90 degrees, counter-clockwise), its length, width and area, from the second moments of its pixels (the sides of the rectangle that has the same moments, exact for a rectangle). The result is a plain file and nothing else is shared with the program that reads it: CSV with the line `index,x_mm,y_mm,angle_deg,length_mm,width_mm,area_mm2` (`--format csv`, the default) or JSON with `format` (`eh3d-vision-parts`), `version` (1), `unit`, the size of the picture, `mm_per_px` and the list `parts` (`--format json`); `measure::parse_csv` reads the CSV back. A spot cut by the edge of the picture is left out, a picture with more spots than `max_parts` is refused with the code `too_many_parts`, and `--output FILE` writes a new file and refuses one that is there (`file_exists`) unless `--replace` is given, and a link even then (`limit::write_text`). The scale comes from `--mm-per-px` or `mm_per_px`, and `part_min_area`, `part_max_area` and `max_parts` are new keys of `vision.cfg` and `examples/vision.cfg`.
- **Uneven light is taken out before the search (`flatten::flatten`, `find --flatten PX`):** a lamp that fades across the board or a shadow made one threshold for the whole picture fail (the dark side was all "mark"). Each pixel is now divided by the mean of the square window around it (from an integral image, so the cost does not grow with the window) and the board comes out about the same grey everywhere; the window is about three times the largest mark. Tested on a board that fades from 250 to 60 (one threshold finds fewer marks than there are, the flattened picture finds all of them) and with light marks on a dark board. The new key `flatten_window` (0, off, by default; 3 to 4001) is in `vision.cfg` and `examples/vision.cfg`.
- **The centre of a mark to a fraction of a pixel, with a confidence (`fiducial::refine`, `find --refine`):** the first centre is the middle of the pixels that passed the threshold, which moves by up to half a pixel with the way the edge falls on the pixels; the refined centre weighs each pixel near the mark by how much darker (or lighter) than the board it is, so the grey pixels of the edge count for what they hold (tested on discs with a soft edge, in both polarities, to 0.06 pixel). The confidence is the score of the shape, lowered when the mark stands out only a little from the board. The new keys `refine_subpixel` (no by default, so the output is as before) and `full_contrast` (0.3: the contrast from which the confidence is the score alone) are in `vision.cfg`, and `examples/vision.cfg` lists them.
- **The README says the maturity of the manifest:** the badge and the Maturity row of the eight READMEs read functional, as the manifest does, and the check of the status lights now fails when they differ.
- **`align --pair-nearest` takes the measured marks in any order:** each design mark takes the measured mark that lies nearest to it (once both sets are moved to their own centre), as the window and the simulated camera already do; without the option the rows are paired as written.
- **`align --isocam-args` prints the arguments only for a fit it accepts:** the lines `--fiducial ...` used to be printed before the residual and the stretch were checked, so a script that captured the output received the arguments of a rejected fit and only the exit code told the difference; the check now comes first. `--max-rms` and `--max-stretch` must also be numbers that are not negative, as in the settings. Tested with a mark 5 mm off.
- **A simulated camera over a board (`sim`):** a host twin that draws the fiducial marks of a design where a known turn and shift put them, with the faults of a camera on purpose (noise, a frame too bright or too dark, a dead sensor, a flat frame, dust the size of a mark, a frame that never arrives, a picture file cut before its end), the glue that finds the marks, counts them against the design, pairs them and fits the transform (refusing a count that does not match and a poor fit), and a retry that asks for another frame. Seven new tests (153 in all) check that the turn and the shift that drew the frame come back, that the same seed repeats exactly, that bad frames are refused and the next good one recovers, that a picture file cut at any place is refused, and that noise strong enough to defeat the automatic threshold (no mark is found) is refused and a threshold given by hand recovers. It has only run on drawn marks: no camera, lens or board was used.

## [0.0.4] - Picture mode with scale and alignment and size-limited file reads

- **The shared validation tools are updated:** the validator also runs the workflow and lessons checks of the suite with their latest rules, including a check to run before every push.
- **The CI validation checks the workflows and the lessons of past CI failures against a baseline:** `tools/workflow_hygiene.py` and `tools/lessons_lint.py` (copies of the shared tools) run in the validation, and only findings that are not in `tools/workflow_hygiene_baseline.json` and `tools/lessons_lint_baseline.json` fail it, so what exists today stays as it is and a new one is caught. `python tools/workflow_hygiene.py --root . --detail` and `python tools/lessons_lint.py --repo .` list the findings.
- **The validation of the CI is the reusable one of the documentation hub:** the job `validate` calls `validate.yml` of EH3D-DOCS (pinned by commit) and the check of the CHANGELOG is a job of its own.
- **The command line reads files with a size limit:** `eh3d-vision find` and `align` read the picture and the files of points only when they are not larger than `max_file_mib` of `vision.cfg` (64 MiB by default, the same value the window uses), reading at most one byte more than the limit even if the file grows meanwhile. A refusal starts with a code that does not change: `file_too_large`, `file_unreadable` or `file_not_text`, followed by the file name. The reader is `limit::read_limited` / `read_text_limited`, with `tests/read_limit.rs`.
- **The lines of the alignment in the window:** once the design is aligned with the marks, the window draws for each design point a line from the place the design puts it to the mark the camera found for it, green when the distance is within `max_rms` and red when it is not, with the distance in millimetres beside it (`Window::residuals`, worked out in millimetres and handed to the drawing in pixels, with its tests). The new key `show_residuals` of `vision.cfg` (yes by default, listed in `examples/vision.cfg`) switches them off.
- **The picture mode can show the scale and the alignment:** `--shot FILE.png --mark N --align` sets the scale from mark number N (from 1) and aligns the design with the marks before the picture is taken, so the picture shows the millimetres of the marks, the crosses of the design and the lines of the alignment; a number that is not a mark, or an alignment that cannot be made, ends the run with the reason instead of a picture of another state, and both options need `--shot`. `examples/design_pair.csv` is a design for the two marks of `examples/marks.pgm`.
- **A picture mode for the window:** `eh3d-vision-gui --shot FILE.png` (built with `--features gui`) opens the window at the size of `--size WxH` (320 to 8192, 1180x760 by default), with `--theme forge|dark|light`, `--lang CODE` and the picture and design files of `--open FILE`, waits `--wait N` frames (0 to 3600, 6 by default), saves what it drew as a PNG file and closes by itself. An existing file is never replaced unless `--replace` is given, the window starts from the default values and reads no `vision.cfg` in this mode, the exit code is 0 when the file was written, 1 when it was not (also when the picture does not come back within 600 frames) and 2 for wrong arguments. The arguments are read by plain code with its tests.

## [0.0.3] - The window that finds, calibrates and aligns fiducial marks, with its configuration file

- **A window, `eh3d-vision-gui` (feature `gui`):** it opens a PNG or PGM picture (or takes it dropped on it) with zoom and pan, finds the marks with the threshold, the size, the fill and the elongation as editable values and draws them with their coordinates, sets the scale from the selected mark of a known size, fits the distortion of the lens from a grid of marks, loads the design from a CSV file, shows the turn, the shift, the size and the residual of the alignment, and exports the options `--fiducial` of ISOCAM only when the alignment is accepted. It is a program of its own that opens without a console on Windows and has the icon of the program; the command line `eh3d-vision` is unchanged and a build without the feature has no window code at all. Everything the window decides is in `window.rs`, `viewport.rs`, `lang.rs` and `browse.rs`, tested without a screen; `gui.rs` only draws.
- **The window speaks the eight languages** (English, Spanish, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Chinese and the Portuguese of Portugal), from `lang.rs`; a test checks that every sentence has a text in each one with the same places for the values.
- **A configuration file, `vision.cfg`:** the polarity, the threshold, the range of the diameter, the fill, the elongation, the size of the mark that sets the scale, the columns and rows of the grid, whether the alignment may change the size, the limits of an accepted alignment, the language and the largest file read are written in a `key = value` file (beside the program, in the folder where it starts, or the one named by `EH3D_VISION_CONFIG`) instead of in the code. A key that is unknown or written twice, or a value that makes no sense, is refused with its line; `examples/vision.cfg` lists every key with its default, and a test keeps the file and the defaults equal.
- **The distortion of the lens from a grid of marks (`grid.rs`):** the marks of a target in rows and columns are put in order, the lattice that fits them is worked out and the `k1` that makes them most like that lattice is searched; the residual before and after is reported.
- **The marks are paired with the design by their places (`align::pair_nearest`):** the order in which the camera finds the marks no longer has to be the order of the design; a missing or extra mark is an error.
- **A fixed threshold (`fiducial::find_with`):** the search can take a grey level between dark and light instead of the one of Otsu.
- **The files of points and the options of ISOCAM are in the library (`points::parse`, `align::isocam_args`):** the command line uses them, so the window and `eh3d-vision align --isocam-args` write the same lines.
- **Binaries for every system:** the workflow `release-binaries.yml` builds `eh3d-vision` and `eh3d-vision-gui` for Windows (64 and 32 bits), Linux and macOS (Apple silicon and Intel) with their checksums and attestation, and the Rust workflow also lints the window (`--features gui`). The Rust toolchain is pinned in `rust-toolchain.toml` and the icon and the version information are put into the Windows executable.
- **128 tests** run on a computer.
- **The marks for ISOCAM:** `align --isocam-args` prints each pair of marks as the option `--fiducial` of `eh3d-isocam`.
- **The application icon and the logos of the program:** `images/brand/` holds the icon (SVG, PNG sizes, `.ico` and `.icns`), the horizontal and vertical logos in a light and a dark version and the social preview image.
- **Shared checks updated:** the validator also compares the badge and the Version row of the README with the manifest, fails when one of its checks cannot run, and the CHANGELOG step of the workflow reads the base branch from the environment.
- **The reader of pictures comes from EH3D-COMMON (`eh3d-image`):** the program no longer keeps its own copy of the PGM and PNG reader; it takes it by path from the sibling repository, which must be cloned beside this one. `eh3d_*::image` keeps working as before. The manifest names EH3D-COMMON in `depends_on` and the CI checks it out.
- **More tests:** transforms that undo each other, a mirrored or mixed-up board refused, marks found in a noisy picture to a fraction of a pixel, and files of points with comments, Windows line ends and bad lines.
- **The workflows are hardened:** every action is pinned by its commit with its version in a comment, and the checkouts keep no credentials.
- **More tests of the limits:** the fit of the board with two points, points in a line and a half turn, the messages of the acceptance, the scale and the lens.

## [0.0.2] - Shared argument helper and README tests

- **Strict command line:** an option that is written wrong (`--powr`) or a word too many is an error that names it; before, it was ignored and the output was made with the default values.
- **One shared helper for the command-line arguments** (`src/cli_args.rs`: flag, switch, num and a strict finish), the same file in every small utility of the suite; the copies this program had behaved the same, so nothing changes for a person.
- **Tests that run what the README and the scripts promise:** the sample of `run.sh`, `run.bat` and the README is the same command, and it is run on the files of `examples/`.
- **The banner of the README** shows the role and the technologies of this program (it showed the text of the shared library).

## [0.0.1] - Fiducial marks in a camera image: detection, scale and the correction of a board

- **The marks:** Otsu threshold, connected spots, size, fill and elongation checks, marks cut by the edge left out, dark on light or light on dark.
- **The scale and the lens:** millimetres from a mark of known size, y going up, and the radial distortion recovered by least squares and corrected.
- **The board:** the turn, the shift and (if allowed) the size from two or more fiducials, with the residual; a wrong mark or a stretched board is not accepted.
- **The command line** `eh3d-vision` with `find` and `align`, and a sample picture and two sample files of points.
- **13 tests** run on a computer, with damaged input.
